    About BinBeat

    What is real, simulated, and planned

    Who is this for?

    Cleaners / collectors get a prioritised round that skips bins that don't need emptying yet, reducing unnecessary trips. The Campus Map page shows the shortest route between all bins that need attention.

    Students can use Campus Map → Student mode to find the nearest bin that isn't full — handy on the way to the cafeteria or after class.

    What is real vs simulated?

    PartStatus
    Problem, bins, fill scale 0–3, input features Real – from the BinBeat proposal and planned observations.
    Fill values and bin reset baseline Simulated – placeholders until 7–10 day data is collected.
    Prediction logic Simulated – hand-written rules mimicking a decision tree.
    Campus graph / distances Approximate – estimated walking distances.
    Shortest-path algorithm Real – Dijkstra + greedy nearest-neighbour TSP.
    Real-time IoT sensing Proposed – future sensors would feed live levels into this model.

    Predictions support staff judgement; no personal data is collected.
